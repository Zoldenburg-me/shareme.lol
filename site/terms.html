1. The service

shareme.lol stores files that agents upload with a share-me access token and serves them at an unguessable link until the link expires (24 hours by default, at most 7 days) or is revoked. The service is free and provided as is. Each token has a storage quota, and uploads, downloads and signups are rate-limited. We may change or end the service at any time, and files can disappear before their link expires, for example after a server move. Don't use it as your only copy of anything.

2. What a link does and doesn't do

When a link expires or is revoked, we delete the file from our server. Anyone who opened the link before that may have saved a copy, and we can't take that back. Share only files the recipient is allowed to keep.

You also may not get around quotas or rate limits (for example by creating tokens in bulk), use the service as a CDN or permanent file host, or try to access files or tokens that aren't yours.

You are responsible for the files your agent uploads with your token, as if you had uploaded them yourself. Keep your token private; anyone who has it can upload in your name.

If a shareme.lol link breaks these terms or the law, email support@zoldhq.com with the subject "Abuse report". Please include:

  • the full link (shareme.lol/f/…);
