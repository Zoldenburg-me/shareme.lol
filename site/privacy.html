Privacy policy

How shareme.lol processes personal data under the GDPR (Datenschutzerklärung).

Last updated 6 Oct 2026

1. Controller

Zoldenburg UG (haftungsbeschränkt) i.G., Franz-Josef-Straße 11, 80801 München, Germany. Email: support@zoldhq.com

2. Visiting this website

When you open a page, technical connection data (IP address, date and time, requested address, browser type) is processed to deliver the page and keep the service secure and stable (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Our own server does not record IP addresses; it logs only failed requests, with share links removed.

3. Shared files

share-me lets users upload files with an access token and pass on a link to them. We store the file, its name, size and expiry time on our server. Anyone who has the link can open the file until the link expires (24 hours by default, at most 7 days) or the uploader revokes it. The file is then deleted automatically. Links are random and not listed anywhere.

The person who shares a file decides what it contains and who receives the link. We process uploaded files only to store and deliver them (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR towards uploaders, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR otherwise) and do not look at their content, apart from checking that the file type matches its name.

4. Hosting (Akash Network)

The server that stores shared files runs on computing capacity leased through the Akash Network, a decentralised marketplace for cloud resources. The leased provider operates the hardware and can technically access the data stored on it. Files are kept on that server only until they expire.

5. Content delivery (Cloudflare)

All traffic to shareme.lol passes through Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, which connects to our server through an encrypted tunnel. Cloudflare processes technical connection data (including IP addresses) to deliver, secure and stabilise the website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Cloudflare is certified, and additionally on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR). More: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

6. Cookies

shareme.lol sets no cookies of its own, uses no analytics or tracking, and serves its fonts itself, so your browser contacts no third parties beyond Cloudflare. To protect against abusive access, Cloudflare may set the strictly necessary cookie "__cf_bm" (bot detection, lifetime 30 minutes) and, where applicable, "cf_clearance" (confirmation of a passed security check). These cookies are required for operating the website securely (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) and contain no information for tracking you across websites.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR).

8. Right to lodge a complaint

You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

9. Changes

This privacy policy is as of October 2026 and will be updated when needed.
